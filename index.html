About

I'm Greg Kozma, founder of NorthPoint Innovations. With over 30 years in security systems integration, I've seen what fails in the field versus what works on paper.

Every product I build starts with a real operational problem I've personally experienced. Not a market gap identified from a spreadsheet — a problem I lived, understood, and decided to solve.

My approach is edge-first architecture — processing happens on-device, no unnecessary cloud dependencies, no per-seat licensing, no vendor roadmap controlling your technology. Systems that work the way people actually work.

NorthPoint Innovations is the umbrella for everything I build — from AI surveillance infrastructure to field support platforms to CAD tools built specifically for security installers who need to move fast in the field.
